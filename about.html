About ToolBestie

ToolBestie is an independent website that publishes small, focused tools for developers and writers. Each tool does one job well, runs in your browser and does not need an account.

What we make

  • JSON Viewer & Formatter: Format, validate, minify and explore JSON in a searchable tree. Fixes common syntax errors.
  • Cron Expression Validator: Check cron syntax, read it in plain English and preview the next run times.
  • Word Counter: Count words, characters and sentences, estimate reading time and check text limits.

How the tools work

The tools are written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and run on your device. When you paste JSON or type text, the processing happens in your browser tab. We do not receive it, and we have no database that could store it. This is also why the tools respond instantly, and why they keep working with a slow connection once the page has loaded.

Why we explain as well as calculate

A result is more useful when you know why it came out that way. Every tool page includes the rules behind it, the mistakes people commonly make, and answers to common questions. If something is limited or behaves differently from another tool, we say so on the page. For example, the cron validator notes that standard cron treats day-of-month and day-of-week as an either-or, and the JSON viewer explains why very large numbers lose precision in some views.

How the site is funded

ToolBestie is free to use. We show advertising from Google AdSense to cover hosting and development. Advertising never changes what a tool does with your data. You can read how cookies and ads are handled in our Privacy Policy.

Tell us what is missing

Found a bug, or a tool you would like us to build? Write to toolbestie@gmail.com. Bug reports with an example that reproduces the problem are the quickest to fix. See the contact page for details.
