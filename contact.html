Reporting a bug

The tools run entirely in your browser, so we cannot see the data that caused a problem. To help us reproduce it, please include:

  • the tool and page address,
  • a short, non-sensitive example of the input that caused the problem (remove any private data),
  • what you expected and what happened instead,
  • your browser and device, for example “Chrome 130 on Windows 11”.

Privacy requests

We do not collect your tool input and do not have user accounts, so there is very little personal data to request or delete. If you have a question about your data or our Privacy Policy, email us and we will respond.
