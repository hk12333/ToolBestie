Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information ToolBestie (“we”, “us”) handles when you visit toolbestie.com and use our tools. Our short version: what you type into a tool stays in your browser, and the only third parties involved are the hosting provider and advertising partners described below.

1. What you enter into our tools

The JSON viewer, word counter and cron validator run as JavaScript in your browser. The text, JSON or expressions you enter are processed on your device. They are not sent to our servers, we cannot read them, and we do not store them. Closing or reloading the page clears them.

2. Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our web host may record standard server logs when you request a page, including your IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the date and time. We use this information only to operate and secure the site. We do not currently run analytics software on this site. If we add it, we will update this policy first.

3. Information stored on your device

We use your browser's local storage to remember one preference, your light or dark theme (under the key tb-theme). It stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can clear it in your browser settings at any time.

4. Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertising, which helps keep the tools free. Google and its advertising partners are third-party vendors that use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads.

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a consent message before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy controls shown on the site or in your browser.

We do not control the cookies set by third parties and cannot access them. The advertising providers' own privacy policies apply to the data they collect.

5. How we use information

We use the limited information described above to deliver and secure the website, to understand and fix technical problems, and to show advertising. We do not sell personal information, and we do not build profiles of visitors ourselves.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold almost no personal data, this will usually be limited to server logs kept by our host. Email toolbestie@gmail.com and we will respond. California residents can also opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for advertising through the controls described in section 4.

7. Children's privacy

ToolBestie is a general-audience site and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Links to other websites

Our pages may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please read their policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email toolbestie@gmail.com or visit our contact page.
